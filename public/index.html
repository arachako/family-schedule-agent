Why this is agentic
  1. Observes multiple people’s commitments and constraints.
  2. Detects explicit and implicit conflicts.
  3. Reasons about importance, flexibility, buffers, and logistics.
  4. Produces prioritized recommendations with tradeoffs.
  5. Stops before action. Human remains in control.
